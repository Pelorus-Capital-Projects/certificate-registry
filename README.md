# Certificate Registry

A personal registry for maritime and safety certificates: categories, issue and expiry dates, expiry reminders, the PDF of each certificate, training centre contacts and a profile.

**Open the app:** https://pelorus-capital-projects.github.io/certificate-registry/

## Login

The app opens behind a username and password that you set the first time you open it. Everything it stores in the browser is encrypted (AES-256) and unlocks only with your password or the answer to your recovery question. It locks itself after 10 minutes without use. Each browser and device has its own login, set when you first open the app there.

## Your data

- Nothing you enter is sent anywhere. Certificates, PDFs and your profile are saved in the browser you use, on that device only.
- Each browser and each device keeps its own separate copy. Use **Profile → Backup** to save a backup file regularly, and to load it on another device or browser.
- Backup files are not encrypted: keep them somewhere private.
- This public copy contains the app only: no certificates, documents, passwords or personal details.

## iPhone

Open the link in Safari, tap Share, choose **Add to Home Screen** and keep **Open as Web App** switched on. Then open the app from its Home Screen icon and load your backup there.
